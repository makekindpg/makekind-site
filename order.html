Online order

Pick a design.
Wear it proudly.

Every button ships from purebuttons.com. Click a design below and its order form opens pre-loaded with a starting quantity — most default to 100, though one defaults to 10. Either way, you can change the quantity on their site.

  1. Click any design below. It opens purebuttons.com with that design and a starting quantity already loaded — adjust it up or down however you'd like.

  2. Want to mix designs? Come back to this page and click another one. It adds to the same cart on purebuttons.com instead of starting over, so you can combine styles in one order.

  3. Once your cart totals $35 or more, apply FREESHIP at checkout — it brings USPS Advantage (10+ day) shipping down to $0. Faster shipping options still cost extra.

Note: adding a design keeps your cart on purebuttons.com — just don't check out between visits if you're planning to combine styles.
