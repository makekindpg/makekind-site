Which one is right for you?

Two places, two different jobs

purebuttons.com & stickylife.com

Best if you want to hand buttons out. Button pricing drops sharply at 100 units — that's why the Order Buttons page defaults to a quantity of 100 (car magnets default to 10). If you want a stack to give away, this is where to go.

Redbubble

Best if you want just one — or something that isn't a button or magnet. No minimum order, and a much wider range of products: shirts, stickers, mugs, phone cases, tote bags, and more, all with the same designs.

Both are genuinely non-commercial — I take no commission on either one. The only difference is which one fits what you're trying to do.
